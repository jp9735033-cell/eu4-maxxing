# Same five-slot layout as Yan, Wu and Yue. See EMX_Yan_Missions.txt for the map.
#
# Shu looks west. Its national branch is seven steps of road building across
# Tibet, the Hexi Corridor and the Tarim oases, and each step cancels the
# development penalty of the terrain it crosses: desert +50%, mountain +35%,
# highlands and steppe +20%. Ground nobody else can afford to settle becomes
# Shu territory worth holding.
