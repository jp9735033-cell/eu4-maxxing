# eu4-maxxing: the Yan warring-states mission tree.
#
# Layout, shared by Yan, Wu, Shu and Yue:
#
#   pos  slot 1      slot 2        slot 3        slot 4        slot 5
#   1    Capital 1   Home 1        Home 5        National 1    National 4
#   2    Capital 2   Home 2        Home 6        National 2    National 5
#   3    Capital 3   Home 3        Home 7        National 3    [Shu only]
#   4                Home 4                                    [Shu only]
#   5                Historic 1    Historic 2    Historic 3    Historic 4
#   6                              Unify China
#   7    Silk 1      Army 1        Industry 1    Industry 4
#   8    Silk 2      Army 2        Industry 2    Industry 5
#   9    Silk 3      Army 3        Industry 3
#
# Every column is a plain vertical chain: each mission requires only the one
# directly above it, so the arrows are all straight. The three rival capitals
# are free of each other and can be taken in any order. The four historic
# missions at row 5 all hang off Home 4 and none require each other.
# Unify China at row 6 has no mission prerequisites at all - it is gated
# purely by its own trigger, owning and coring the four player capitals and
# Shenyang. The three lines below it still branch from it.
#
# Yan holds Beijing and the Mandate of Heaven. Its tree is about the northern
# frontier: the wall, the garrisons and the steppe beyond them.
